THE MOGUL

A simple wheel arrangement with a long history.

A Mogul is a steam locomotive with a 2-6-0 wheel arrangement: two leading wheels followed by six coupled driving wheels.

The arrangement gave railroads a useful combination of flexibility and adhesion. For the Boston & Maine, Moguls became part of the everyday machinery of New England railroading.

PRESERVED HISTORY

No. 1455

Boston & Maine No. 1455 was built by the American Locomotive Company at its Manchester, New Hampshire works in 1907.

The locomotive represents a direct connection between B&M's steam-era operations and the industrial history of New Hampshire.

After its railroad career, 1455 passed through preservation collections and ultimately became part of the Danbury Railway Museum collection.
